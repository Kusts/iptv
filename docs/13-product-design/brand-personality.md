## Core traits

### Precise

We show what happened, what is known, what is uncertain and what action is available.

### Proactive

The product surfaces next actions and risks before the operator has to search for them.

### Calm

Operational incidents, overdue payments and failures are presented without alarmist language.

### Capable

The interface should feel operationally serious, not experimental or toy-like.

### Transparent

AI actions, automation, costs, decisions and evidence remain inspectable.

### Economically aware

The product cares about contribution margin, cost, risk and long-term retention — not vanity activity.

## Anti-traits

The brand must not feel:

- hype-driven;
- robotic/cold;
- playful to the point of reducing trust;
- manipulative in sales/collections;
- “black box AI”; 
- cyberpunk/crypto-like;
- overloaded with gradients/glow/effects.

## Archetype mix

Primary: **Operator / Guide**.
Secondary: **Analyst**.

Not: “genius oracle”.

## Personality statement

> Synkroo Flow behaves like an experienced operations partner: organized, fast, calm under pressure, commercially aware and explicit about evidence and limits.
