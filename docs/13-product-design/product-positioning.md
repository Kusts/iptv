## Category

**AI Revenue & Operations OS for subscription businesses.**

The product is not positioned as a chatbot builder. It is an operating platform that connects acquisition, trial, commerce, fulfillment, support, retention, referral, finance and learning.

## Initial vertical

The first vertical is a digital subscription/streaming operation used as the pilot. The platform must remain provider-agnostic and must not assume that the future SaaS customer uses CINEVISION or the same offer catalog.

## Primary promise

> Run the customer lifecycle from lead to renewal in one operating system, with AI that can act inside rules, humans in control of exceptions, and every outcome feeding better decisions.

## Target user

Initial operator profile:

- small or medium subscription operation;
- sales/support heavily dependent on WhatsApp/social channels;
- recurring billing and renewals;
- operational work spread across provider panels, spreadsheets and chat history;
- low visibility into unit economics, churn, attribution and support knowledge;
- founder/operator still performing repetitive operational work manually.

## Jobs to be done

1. Know what needs attention now.
2. Convert leads without losing context.
3. Qualify technical fit before charging.
4. Collect payment and fulfill reliably.
5. Resolve support with reusable knowledge.
6. Renew and recover customers systematically.
7. Grow referrals without losing margin.
8. Understand real profit, not only revenue.
9. Let AI operate safely without giving it unlimited authority.
10. Turn operational outcomes into learning.

## Differentiation

The durable differentiation is the loop:

`Conversation → Action → Outcome → Measurement → Learning → Better Decision`.

The product combines operational state, economic state, AI execution, provider fulfillment, evidence and analytics. A generic CRM/chatbot normally owns only a subset.

## Positioning guardrails

Do not market the product as:

- “fully autonomous, no humans needed”;
- a way to bypass platform policies;
- a generic AI agent builder;
- a replacement for accounting/legal responsibility;
- a promise of guaranteed revenue.

## Elevator pitch

**Synkroo Flow is an AI-native revenue and operations platform for recurring-revenue businesses. It unifies CRM, trials, billing, fulfillment, support, renewals, referrals and profitability, while keeping sensitive actions governed by deterministic rules and human approval.**
