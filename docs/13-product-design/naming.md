# Naming Strategy

> Status: Proposed — working brand selected, legal clearance pending
> Versão: 0.13
> Data: 2026-09-20
> Review: Auto-reviewed v0.13 — external collision research considered; no trademark conclusion implied.

## Naming requirements

The name should:

- remain valid beyond the initial streaming vertical;
- fit under the existing Synkroo brand ecosystem;
- suggest movement/operations rather than “chatbot”;
- be easy to pronounce in Portuguese;
- support a white-label SaaS later;
- work visually in a B2B control center;
- avoid names already strongly occupied in AI/CRM/operations software.

## Rejected external-style candidates

During research, names similar to **Operly**, **Klyro**, **Auvora** and **Synkora** were found already in active use by software/AI companies. They must not be used as our product name.

## Finalists

| Candidate | Strength | Risk | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Synkroo Flow** | strongly tied to parent brand; suggests continuous operations and automation | “Flow” is generic by itself | **Recommended working name** |
| Synkroo Pulse | strong analytics/health metaphor | narrower, sounds monitoring-heavy | Alternative |
| Synkroo Orbit | ecosystem/orchestration metaphor | less direct about revenue operations | Alternative |
| Synkroo OS | very descriptive | weak trademarkability and generic | Internal descriptor only |

## Recommended working brand

# Synkroo Flow

Short form: **Flow**.

Descriptor:

> AI Revenue & Operations OS

## Product/agent naming relationship

- Product: **Synkroo Flow**
- Internal short name: **Flow**
- Default pilot agent display name: **Flow Assist**
- SaaS tenants may override customer-facing agent display name while keeping policy/behavior boundaries.

## Legal/public launch gate

Before public branding is frozen:

1. search INPI/trademark classes relevant to software/SaaS;
2. check desired domains/social handles;
3. conduct legal naming review;
4. verify no confusingly similar local mark;
5. only then move naming ADR from `Proposed` to `Accepted`.

A web search that finds no obvious exact-name collision is not legal clearance.
