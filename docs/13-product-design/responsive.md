## Breakpoint intent

Do not design by fixed device names only. Use layout capacity.

### Wide desktop

- persistent navigation;
- split panes;
- dense tables;
- side evidence panels.

### Standard laptop/tablet landscape

- collapsible navigation;
- one primary workspace + drawers;
- reduced nonessential columns.

### Mobile

- attention/inbox/customer quick actions first;
- tables become cards or drill-down lists;
- no horizontal scrolling for critical workflows;
- bulk operations and complex financial configuration can be desktop-recommended.

## Touch targets

Minimum interactive size should meet common accessibility guidance; critical actions must not be tightly adjacent.
