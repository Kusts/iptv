## Items for validation

- lawful basis and notices for customer/lead data;
- marketing communication/opt-out requirements;
- retention periods by data category;
- invoice/payment/accounting record retention;
- deletion/anonymization exceptions;
- cross-border processors/subprocessors;
- rights/licensing for content used in marketing;
- SaaS terms/privacy/DPA when product is sold to other tenants;
- managerial revenue recognition vs statutory accounting treatment;
- tax implications of rewards/discounts where applicable.

## Output required

Each item must receive: jurisdiction, responsible advisor, conclusion, date, implementation requirement and retention/policy change if any.
