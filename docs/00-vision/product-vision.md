# Product Vision — AI Revenue & Operations Platform

> Status: **FINAL v1.0**  
> Date: 2026-09-26

## Vision

Build an AI-first operating system for recurring businesses that can acquire, activate, sell, fulfill, support, retain, recover and expand customer relationships while maintaining authoritative business state, economic visibility and human control for true exceptions.

The internal streaming/subscription operation is the first normal tenant, laboratory and case study. It must not receive architectural shortcuts that external tenants cannot use.

## Product promise

The platform should make a small operation feel operationally larger without requiring a large human team. The AI performs the majority of routine work, but every meaningful action remains governed by deterministic business rules, permissions, policies, durable workflows, audit and recoverability.

## Economic flywheel

`Acquisition → Activation/Trial → Sale → Fulfillment → Retention → Expansion → Referral → Recovery → Acquisition`

The system optimizes lifetime contribution and cohort payback rather than first-order margin in isolation.

## Distribution flywheel

`Acquire reseller → educate → first sale → grow portfolio → create sub-resellers → adopt SaaS → operate better → resell SaaS → grow ecosystem`.

Reseller growth should increase master contribution and create a natural SaaS acquisition channel.

## SaaS evolution

The pilot proves operational value first. External SaaS pricing, packaging and limits are then designed from real usage/cost/value telemetry rather than guesses.

## Non-goals

The product is not a generic chatbot, a WhatsApp blaster, a provider-panel clone, a full accounting ERP, an event-sourcing experiment or an excuse to automate unsafe/unauthorized actions. External systems are adapters; the owned backend remains the source of truth.
