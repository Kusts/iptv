# Partners, Resellers and Distribution

## Business model

A reseller is simultaneously a B2B customer, distribution channel, independent commercial operation and potential SaaS tenant/reseller. The platform should help the reseller grow because its growth increases master contribution and creates SaaS opportunities.

## Hierarchy

`Master → Direct Reseller → Sub-reseller → ...`

- each partner operationally manages only direct children;
- ancestors may view authorized network aggregates;
- direct operational access to descendants requires explicit delegated access;
- hierarchy is modeled with direct parent-child relationships; depth is derived.

## SaaS roles

A PartnerAccount may carry independent capabilities such as `SERVICE_RESELLER` and future `SAAS_RESELLER`, while also linking to a `Tenant` when the reseller becomes a SaaS customer.

## Reseller lifecycle

`PROSPECT → ONBOARDING → TRAINING → READY → ACTIVE`, with `AT_RISK | INACTIVE | SUSPENDED | TERMINATED` alternatives.

## Education as product

The acquisition process includes education so the reseller can sell and support independently. MVP Academy covers:

1. product/service;
2. devices/apps/connections;
3. Trials and legitimate Retrial;
4. provisioning;
5. technical support/escalation;
6. consultative sales/objections/follow-up;
7. renewal/retention;
8. basic finance/pricing;
9. campaigns/acquisition;
10. pathway to using the SaaS.

Learning content is curated/versioned; progress is tracked without making training unnecessary bureaucracy.

## Economics

MVP starts prepaid/wholesale with a reseller credit ledger (`AVAILABLE → RESERVED → CONSUMED/RELEASED`). Prices, tiers and campaigns are versioned/configurable. Postpaid credit limits are post-MVP.

Master contribution measures reseller revenue less provider cost, fees, support, discounts and campaign subsidies; acquisition decisions use lifetime contribution/payback, not first purchase alone.

## SaaS growth loop

`Reseller grows → operational complexity grows → SaaS opportunity → reseller becomes Tenant → operates better → can grow network further → may later resell SaaS`.

SaaS adoption is incentivized but not artificially required for the reseller relationship unless future policy has a legitimate reason.
