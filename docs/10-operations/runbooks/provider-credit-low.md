# Runbook — Provider Credit Low / Recharge Deadline

> Status: Operational baseline  
> Version: 1.0  
> Review: Auto-reviewed v0.12 — checked against inventory ledger, forecast and supplier-account continuity requirements.

## Trigger

- projected credits below safety stock;
- days-of-inventory below threshold;
- supplier recharge deadline approaching;
- unexpected burn-rate spike.

## Procedure

1. verify ledger balance vs provider-observed balance;
2. reconcile unexplained drift;
3. inspect renewal/new-sale forecast and active incidents;
4. compare available supplier packages/contract economics;
5. raise purchase recommendation/approval according to policy;
6. record CreditPurchase and resulting inventory when completed;
7. update forecast.

## 45-day continuity rule

The supplier-reported periodic recharge requirement remains an operational policy input; exact enforcement/terms must be revalidated if provider terms change.

## Auto-review result

Reviewed to connect procurement action to authoritative inventory and forecast instead of provider balance alone.
